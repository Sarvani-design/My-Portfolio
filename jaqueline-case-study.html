Design Decisions

Two calls worth explaining

Why a language switcher?

The shop already serves English and Spanish speakers daily — the site should too. A direct line to more bookings, not a checkbox.

Why source from Google Maps?

Name, reviews, rating, hours, address — all copied from the real listing, so the concept is grounded in a real, findable business.

Reflection

What this taught me

  • The bilingual toggle was the whole point. Not a trend — it came straight from how this shop actually serves its customers.
  • These are stock photos, not the shop's own. Version one uses placeholder images; real photography from the owner is the obvious next step.
  • It hasn't been adopted yet. Pitched, not confirmed — an unsolicited redesign is a different ask than a brief.

Design Decisions

One call worth explaining

Why show real prices instead of hiding them?

Price is one of the first things people check before deciding where to eat. Showing every dish's ₹ price up front turns "maybe" into "let's go" — instead of making them wait until they're already there to find out.

Why source from Google Maps?

Name, reviews, rating, address — copied from the real listing, grounding the site in a business with 2,373 reasons to trust it.

Reflection

What this taught me

  • Real data beats a blank prompt. An actual Maps listing and a reference template got further, faster, than imagination.
  • 2,373 reviews is its own kind of content. The number does persuasive work before a single quote is read.
  • It hasn't been adopted yet. Pitched, not confirmed — an unsolicited redesign is a different ask than a brief.
